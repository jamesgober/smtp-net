# smtp-net v0.4.0 - Extended

**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Compare:** [`v0.3.0...v0.4.0`](https://github.com/jamesgober/smtp-net/compare/v0.3.0...v0.4.0)

**TLS relays, AUTH, and the job path.** v0.4.0 took smtp-net from the local mail server to remote relays: STARTTLS on 587 and implicit TLS on 465 with certificate verification, AUTH PLAIN and LOGIN, CHUNKING, DSN, a pacing gate for host limits, and a checksummed job payload so a worker sends exactly the bytes the request handler built.

## What's in 0.4.0

- **TLS.** The driver ran STARTTLS and implicit TLS through a `TlsConnector` seam over net-io's sans-IO TLS session. With feature `tls`, on by default, the engine was tls-net's client (rustls, Mozilla roots), shared across servers so sessions could resume; `ServerBuilder::tls` took a client that trusts a private CA. Verification could not be turned off with tls-net; certificate problems were classified permanent, handshake I/O failures temporary. A stripped STARTTLS ended the session with nothing sent (T-5), plaintext behind the `220` was refused (T-6).
- **AUTH.** PLAIN and LOGIN from a caller allowlist in the caller's order, never a fallback outside it (T-10); credentials only over TLS or to a verified loopback peer (T-8); passwords wiped on drop and redacted everywhere, the AUTH output overwritten once sent (T-9). `454` was temporary, `432`, `530`, `534`, `535`, `538` permanent.
- **CHUNKING and DSN.** BDAT sent the bytes as they are, pipelined with MAIL and RCPT (one round trip), and waited for the recipient replies above 64 KiB. DSN parameters (`RET`, `ENVID`, `NOTIFY`, `ORCPT`, xtext-encoded) went out when offered, or refused the send when required and absent.
- **Deferral.** Recipients refused as too many for one transaction (`452 4.5.3`) went in further transactions on the same connection with the same bytes.
- **Pacing.** `SendGate` was asked before connecting and before each transaction; `HourlyLimit` counted messages or recipients over a rolling hour without locks and never admitted more than its limit; a refusal was temporary with `retry_after`.
- **Job payloads.** `PreparedMessage::encode` and `decode` carried the envelope and bytes with a version and a CRC-32C; every flipped bit was refused (T-33); `send_prepared` sent them without re-serializing.
- **Messages.** `List-Unsubscribe` with one-click (RFC 8058), `Auto-Submitted`, dates with a zone offset, and a public `Part` tree for callers who build MIME themselves.

## Fixed

- `Address` equality compared only the domain in 0.3.0, so two addresses at the same domain were equal.
- `MessageBuilder::date_unix` was ignored in 0.3.0.

## Verification

- 60 transcripts whole and split into 1 to 16 octet pieces; driver tests for TLS, AUTH, BDAT, DSN, deferral, pacing, and payloads against `TestServer`; real-TLS tests with rustls on both sides and fixture certificates; a job-contract test (temporary to retry, permanent to dead letter); loom models for `HourlyLimit`.
- CI: Linux, macOS, Windows on stable and 1.85; bare-metal `no_std`; fuzz smoke runs of nine targets; interop with Mailpit (plaintext, STARTTLS, implicit TLS with verification) and Exim.

## What's next

`0.5.0`: DKIM signing (Ed25519-SHA256 and RSA-SHA256 through crypt-io 1.3), XOAUTH2, SCRAM-SHA-256, the connection pool, the observer hook, streaming attachments, and the deliverability guide.
